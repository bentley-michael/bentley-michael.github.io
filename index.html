About

Practical automation, API integration, and technical operations

I'm Michael Bentley. I build practical automations and API integrations for small businesses and teams that need tools connected, repetitive work reduced, or incoming data routed reliably.

I work directly with n8n workflows, webhooks, REST APIs, authentication, structured data handling, FastAPI services, testing, and documentation. AnsweredCall applies those skills to voice intake and lead routing for home-service businesses.

My approach is direct: identify the bottleneck, design the flow, build what matters, and deliver a system that is understandable, testable, and straightforward to hand off.

What working together looks like

  • Start from a specific business problem, not a technology wishlist
  • Design for lead routing accuracy, response speed, and workflow consistency
  • Keep the architecture lean, observable, and appropriate to the scope
  • Prioritize handoff clarity and long-term maintainability over novelty
