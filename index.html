Featured Build

Featured System: AnsweredCall

A customer-facing automation offer built around voice intake, structured lead capture, and immediate operational alerts for home-service businesses.

How it works

01
A caller reaches the voice intake The voice agent handles the conversation and collects the information needed for follow-up.
02
The call becomes structured data Contact, location, service, urgency, and call-summary fields are normalized into a predictable schema.
03
n8n applies routing rules The workflow filters junk calls and prepares qualified leads for storage and notification.
04
The lead is recorded and surfaced Qualified lead data is written to Google Sheets and sent to Discord as an operational alert.
05
Configuration stays outside the workflow Client-specific sheet, alert, and filtering settings are loaded from environment configuration.
